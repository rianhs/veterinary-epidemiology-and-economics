Each competency of the course is made of small skills. Learn a skill after its prerequisites. Codes that start with X are skills from earlier courses, such as Biostatistics.

| Depth | Meaning |
|-|-|
| Core | You perform it alone |
| Applied | You perform it with a template, a tool, or guidance |
| Introduced | You recognize and interpret it |
