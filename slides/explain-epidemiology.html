Explain epidemiology

Why does a veterinarian examine the entire flock when a single hen is sick?

  • S1.1Explain epidemiology and its population perspective
  • TimeAbout 15 minutes
Read the guiding question aloud. Ask the class for a first answer in one sentence. Students will return to this answer at the end of the deck.

The scenario: Salmonella on a layer farm

In one week, 120 of the 8,000 hens on a layer farm die. Most of the dead hens come from the two pens, which are located near the water inlet.

The laboratory later finds Salmonella in the dead hens. Eggs from these pens have already reached buyers.

You are the production animal veterinarian of this farm.

Ask the class what they would do first. Most students name the necropsy. Use that answer on the next slide.

You do a necropsy on five hens. Does the result of necropsy inform the farm owner what to do?

Choose your prediction.

The simple definition of epidemiology

Epidemiology is the study of health-related events in populations.

Hover over the two dotted terms. Ask: which other health-related events could the veterinarian count on this farm? Accept answers such as a fall in egg production.

Clinical question and population question

The same disease on the farm raises two kinds of question. A clinical question concerns one animal. A population question concerns a group of animals.

Clinical question: one animal

  • What is wrong with this hen?
  • Which treatment does this hen need?

Population question: a group

  • How many hens died, and where?
  • What do the sick hens share?

The veterinarian of the farm needs the answers to both kinds of question.

Point to the left panel, then to the right panel. Ask which panel the necropsy belongs to. Then ask which panel tells the farm where to act first.

The full definition of epidemiology

Epidemiology is the study of the distribution and determinants of health-related states or events in specified populations, and the application of this study to the control of health problems.

Source of the definition: Last (2001).

Read the definition in three parts. After each part, ask a student to explain it in simple words. Point out that the simple definition on the last slide is a short form of this sentence.

The six elements of epidemiology in the layer farm

The full definition of epidemiology has six elements. Each row of the table takes one element and shows what it is on the layer farm.

ElementIn the layer farm
StudyThe veterinarian works from records and counts.
Health-related states or eventsDeaths of hens and a fall in egg production.
Specified populationsThe 8,000 hens in 8 pens.
Distribution90 of the 120 deaths were in pens A1 and B1, in one week.
DeterminantsPens A1 and B1 are located near the water inlet, making water a factor that required testing. The laboratory finds Salmonella from dead hens.
ApplicationThe veterinarian separates the two pens, treats the hens, cleans the water supply, and finds the buyers of the eggs.
For each row, ask the class where they saw this fact on the Scenario slide. Ask which element the manager had already used before the veterinarian arrived. A good answer is distribution, because the manager had counted the deaths.

Clinician vs epidemiologist

A clinician and an epidemiologist can work on the same disease, such as Salmonella on the layer farm. The table compares their work in four parts.

Part of the workClinicianEpidemiologist
FocusIndividualsPopulations
ObjectiveCuring the sick individualPreventing and controlling disease in populations
DiagnosisBased on anamnesis, physical examination, and diagnostic testsBased on statistical analysis and epidemiological studies
TreatmentGiving medical or surgical treatment and careImplementing interventions and policies that affect the population
Ask which row describes the necropsy and which row describes the count by pen.

Find the error

A student summarises the scenario of the layer farm in one note.

"A veterinarian did a necropsy on five hens. The laboratory result showed Salmonella. These findings show the epidemiology of the problem."

What information is missing from the note?

The note describes the cause in five hens. It has no distribution, no determinants, and no application.

The veterinarian separates the two pens with many deaths from the other pens. Which element of the definition is this?

A new scenario: sick dogs in one neighbourhood

Several dogs in one neighbourhood of Makassar have vomiting and bloody diarrhoea in one week. A companion animal veterinarian in a clinic treats each dog.

Which question is a population question?

Key points

  • Epidemiology is the study of health-related events in populations.
  • The full definition of epidemiology adds distribution and determinants, as well as the application of this study.
  • A clinical question concerns one animal. A population question concerns a group of animals or the animal products that come from it.
  • The same disease raises both kinds of question, and the veterinarian needs both.
