The case: a layer farm in South Sulawesi

The farm loses 120 of its 8,000 hens in one week, mostly in two pens next to the water inlet. The laboratory later finds Salmonella in the hens, and eggs from the same pens have already reached buyers.

Your role: production animal veterinarian (GP1.2), two years after graduation.

One disease raises two kinds of question

A clinical question concerns one animal. A population question concerns a group of animals and the pattern of health events in it.

ComparisonClinicianEpidemiologist
FocusIndividualsPopulations
ObjectiveCuring the sick individualPreventing and controlling disease in populations
DiagnosisBased on anamnesis, physical examination, and diagnostic imagingBased on statistical analysis and epidemiological studies
TreatmentGiving medical or surgical treatment and carePutting animal health and public health interventions and policies into practice

In the layer house, the necropsy suggests a cause. The count by pen shows where to act.

Is it a clinical question or a population question?

Ask: does the question concern one animal or a group of animals?

Read each statement aloud and ask the class to vote before you reveal the answer. Statement 5 about the buyers of eggs is the one that students often place wrongly. Ask why eggs belong to the population.

The definition has six elements

Epidemiology is the study of the distribution and determinants of health-related states or events in specified populations, and the application of this study to the control of health problems.

A new case: sick dogs in one neighbourhood

Several dogs in one neighbourhood of Makassar develop vomiting and bloody diarrhoea in one week. A clinic veterinarian (GP1.1) treats each dog. This scenario is synthetic and is for teaching.

Which question is a population question?

Key points

  • Epidemiology studies the distribution and determinants of health-related events in specified populations, and applies the results to control health problems.
  • A clinical question concerns one animal. A population question concerns a group of animals or the animal products that come from it.
  • The same disease raises both kinds of question, and the veterinarian needs both.
  • The six elements of the definition follow the steps of an investigation, from the first count to the action.
