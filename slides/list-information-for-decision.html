The case: a testing service for layer farms

In a district with 60 layer farms, one farm has Salmonella-positive hens, and the number of positive farms is not known. The owner of a small veterinary service company wants to know whether to offer the farms a paid testing and cleaning package.

Your role: veterinary entrepreneur (GP4.2), two years after graduation. You already wrote the problem frame, and now you must list what you need to know.

These numbers are synthetic and are for teaching.

Layer farms in the district
60
Farms known to be positive
1
Options
Full package, test only, no service
Objective in the frame
Contain the problem, find the source
Show the frame from the previous deck in three sentences. Ask the class which things they would want to know before they invest in a service.

You start a list of information for the decision. What makes an item worth listing?

Choose your prediction.

An information need has an item and a possible source

An information need names one item that the decision requires and a place where the item could come from. Sources fall into three types.

Source typeExamples in the caseUsual strength and limit
Existing records and documentsFarm register, laboratory price list, mortality records of the farmsFast and cheap. The data were collected for another purpose
New data collectionTests on a sample of farms, a short survey of farm ownersFits the question. It costs more and takes more time
Literature and expert advicePublished studies on cleaning, advice of a poultry specialistFast and cheap. It may not fit local conditions

Each part of the frame raises information needs

Move through the five parts. Ask students to add one more item to each part. The list is a draft, and it grows and shrinks as the work continues.

Which source type is the practical first choice?

Ask: does the information exist in a file, must someone collect it, or has it been studied elsewhere?

In the case, the district livestock service keeps a register of farms and their sizes. Ask students what changes if no register exists.

Which items does the decision need?

Tick the items for your list in the controls, then check the list. A needed item could change the choice between the options.

Ask students to defend each tick in one sentence. An item that cannot change the choice is not needed, however interesting it is.

The decision needs the price of one Salmonella test. Which information need is written with a source that fits?

A new case: a small animal clinic

A veterinary entrepreneur (GP4.2) plans a second small animal clinic in a city. She must decide whether to open it in neighbourhood A or in neighbourhood B. This scenario is synthetic and is for teaching.

Which item does this decision need?

Key points

  • An information need is one item that a decision requires, together with a possible source for the item.
  • An item belongs on the list when its answer could change the choice between the options.
  • Sources are existing records, new data collection, and literature or expert advice. Each has its own strength and limit.
  • Each part of the problem frame raises information needs, so the frame is the starting point of the list.
