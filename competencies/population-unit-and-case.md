## S2.1 Identify the epidemiological unit {#s2-1}

```{=html}
<div class="skill-body"><p class="skill-scope">System boundary. A group of animals that share about the same likelihood of exposure, such as a pen, herd, flock, or village (WOAH). The epidemiological, observation, and analysis units: choose each for a problem and recognize when the three differ. Target, source, and study populations: the population of interest, the population that can be reached, and the units actually included.</p>
```

## S2.4 Choose a method to estimate population size {#s2-4}

```{=html}
<div class="skill-body"><p class="skill-scope">Stock and flow: births, deaths, sales, purchases. Structure of a population by age, sex, and density. What the choice depends on: the purpose of the count, who is counted (owned, semi-owned, or unowned animals), the biology and behaviour of the animals, scale in space and time, resources, and ethics. Methods that collect new data: census, area sample, quadrat and transect, capture-mark-recapture, signs of presence, and reports by people. Methods that reuse data: administrative records, earlier studies, and derived estimates. Triangulation, assumptions, and uncertainty of an estimate.</p>
<div class="skill-grid"><div><h3 class="mini-title">Depth</h3><p class="depth depth-core">Core</p></div>
```
